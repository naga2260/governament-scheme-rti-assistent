Scheme Name: Pradhan Mantri Awaas Yojana - Gramin (PMAY-G)

Ministry / Department:
Ministry of Rural Development, Government of India.

Purpose:
PMAY-G supports eligible rural households to construct pucca houses with basic amenities. It focuses on rural households that are houseless or living in kutcha / dilapidated houses.

Benefits:
- Financial assistance for construction of a pucca house as per PMAY-G norms.
- Convergence with other schemes may support toilet construction, drinking water, electricity, LPG, and wage employment.
- Beneficiary selection is based on official rural housing criteria and verification.

Eligibility:
- Household should be in a rural area.
- Household should be houseless or living in kutcha/dilapidated housing as per official eligibility process.
- Household should not already own a pucca house.
- Exclusion checks include motorised vehicle ownership, government employment, income tax payment, high income, certain landholding limits, and other criteria.

Documents Commonly Required:
- Aadhaar Card.
- Job card / bank account details.
- Land or house-site details where applicable.
- Mobile number.
- Beneficiary registration / survey details.

Where to Apply / Check:
- Official portal: https://pmayg.nic.in
- Gram Panchayat.
- Block Development Office / Mandal Parishad Development Office.
- District Rural Development Agency / Rural Housing Department.

Common Citizen Issues:
- Name missing from PMAY-G beneficiary list.
- House sanctioned but installment not received.
- Geo-tagging or inspection pending.
- Bank account or Aadhaar mismatch.
- Local verification dispute.

RTI Department / Office:
Public Information Officer, Ministry of Rural Development. Local records may be held by Gram Panchayat, Block Development Office, District Rural Development Agency, or State Rural Housing Department.

Official Source:
