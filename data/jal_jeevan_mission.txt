Scheme Name: Jal Jeevan Mission

Ministry / Department:
Department of Drinking Water and Sanitation, Ministry of Jal Shakti, Government of India.

Purpose:
Jal Jeevan Mission aims to provide functional household tap connections to rural households.

Benefits:
- Household tap water connection in rural areas where implemented.
- Village water supply infrastructure and source sustainability support.
- Water quality monitoring and community participation.
- Reduces burden of fetching water, especially for women and children.

Eligibility:
- Rural households in villages covered under approved Jal Jeevan Mission plans.
- Implementation depends on state/village water supply scheme and local progress.

Documents Commonly Required:
- Household details.
- Aadhaar or identity proof where required locally.
- Residence details.
- Gram Panchayat records.

Where to Apply:
- Gram Panchayat.
- Village Water and Sanitation Committee.
- Rural water supply department.
- Official dashboard: https://jaljeevanmission.gov.in

Common Citizen Issues:
- Tap connection not provided.
- Water supply irregular.
- Poor water quality.
- Pipeline work incomplete.
- Household name missing from village plan.

RTI Department / Office:
Public Information Officer, Department of Drinking Water and Sanitation. Local records may be held by Gram Panchayat and State Rural Water Supply Department.
