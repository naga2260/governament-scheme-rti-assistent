Scheme Name: PM Vishwakarma

Ministry / Department:
Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises, Government of India.

Purpose:
PM Vishwakarma provides end-to-end support to traditional artisans and craftspeople who work with hands and tools. It supports recognition, skill training, toolkit access, credit, digital transactions, and market linkage.

Benefits:
- Recognition through PM Vishwakarma certificate and ID card.
- Basic training of 5 to 7 days and advanced training of 15 days or more.
- Training stipend of Rs. 500 per day.
- Toolkit incentive up to Rs. 15,000 through e-vouchers at the beginning of basic skill training.
- Collateral-free enterprise development loans up to Rs. 3 lakh in two tranches: Rs. 1 lakh and Rs. 2 lakh.
- Concessional interest rate fixed at 5%, with Government support as per scheme rules.
- Digital transaction incentive of Re. 1 per transaction, up to 100 transactions per month.
- Marketing support including quality certification, branding, e-commerce onboarding, publicity, and value-chain linkage.

Eligibility:
- Traditional artisans and craftspeople engaged in notified trades.
- Covered trades include carpenter, boat maker, blacksmith, goldsmith, potter, sculptor, cobbler/footwear artisan, mason, basket/mat/broom maker, doll and toy maker, barber, garland maker, washerman, tailor, fishing net maker, and others listed in guidelines.
- Enrolment is through CSC with Aadhaar-based biometric authentication and multi-level verification.
- Beneficiaries who completed basic training may access first loan tranche; second tranche depends on use and repayment of first loan, standard account status, digital transactions, or advanced training.

Documents Commonly Required:
- Aadhaar Card.
- Mobile number.
- Bank account details.
- Ration card / family details where required.
- Trade details.
- Additional documents requested during CSC registration or verification.

Where to Apply:
- Official portal: https://pmvishwakarma.gov.in
- Common Service Centre (CSC).
- Local Gram Panchayat / Urban Local Body verification process.

Common Citizen Issues:
- Registration pending at verification stage.
- Trade category not clear.
- Training not scheduled.
- Toolkit incentive not received.
- Loan application pending or rejected.
- Digital incentive not credited.

RTI Department / Office:
Public Information Officer, Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises. Local verification records may be held by Gram Panchayat/Urban Local Body, District Implementation Committee, CSC, or implementing agencies.
