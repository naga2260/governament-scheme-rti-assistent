Scheme Name: National Means-cum-Merit Scholarship Scheme (NMMSS)

Ministry / Department:
Department of School Education and Literacy, Ministry of Education, Government of India.

Purpose:
NMMSS provides scholarships to meritorious students from economically weaker sections to reduce dropout after class 8.

Benefits:
- Annual scholarship support for selected students studying in classes 9 to 12, subject to renewal rules.
- Encourages continuation of secondary education.
- Scholarship is usually disbursed through DBT after verification on the National Scholarship Portal.

Eligibility:
- Student must qualify in the state-level NMMSS selection examination.
- Family income ceiling applies as per scheme rules.
- Student should study in eligible government/government-aided/local body schools.
- Renewal depends on academic performance and continuation in eligible classes.

Documents Commonly Required:
- Aadhaar card.
- Bank account details.
- Income certificate.
- School certificate / bonafide certificate.
- Caste/category certificate where applicable.
- Mark sheets.

Where to Apply:
- National Scholarship Portal: https://scholarships.gov.in
- School and state education department verification channels.

Common Citizen Issues:
- NSP application rejected during school/district/state verification.
- Bank account or Aadhaar mismatch.
- Scholarship not credited.
- Renewal application missed.
- Income certificate issue.

RTI Department / Office:
Public Information Officer, Ministry of Education. Local verification records may be held by school, district education office, and state nodal officer.

Official Source:
https://scholarships.gov.in and https://education.gov.in
