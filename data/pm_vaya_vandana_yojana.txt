Scheme Name: Pradhan Mantri Vaya Vandana Yojana (PMVVY)

Ministry / Department:
Department of Financial Services, Ministry of Finance, Government of India. Implemented through Life Insurance Corporation of India as notified.

Purpose:
PMVVY is a pension scheme for senior citizens providing assured pension returns for the policy term, subject to availability and government notifications.

Benefits:
- Pension payment option for eligible senior citizens as per scheme terms.
- Assured return/pension rate as notified for the scheme period.
- Pension modes may include monthly, quarterly, half-yearly, or yearly options.
- Provides income support to senior citizens.

Eligibility:
- Senior citizens meeting minimum age rules as per scheme terms.
- Purchase price and pension limits apply.
- Scheme availability depends on current government/LIC notification status.

Documents Commonly Required:
- Aadhaar card.
- PAN.
- Age proof.
- Bank account details.
- Photograph.
- Proposal/application form.

Where to Apply:
- LIC branch / LIC online channels where scheme is open.
- Official information: https://licindia.in and https://financialservices.gov.in

Common Citizen Issues:
- Scheme subscription window closed.
- Pension payment delay.
- Bank account update issue.
- Nominee update.
- Confusion about return rate and lock-in.

RTI Department / Office:
Public Information Officer, Department of Financial Services. Policy-specific records are held by LIC.

Official Source:
https://financialservices.gov.in and https://licindia.in
