Scheme Name: Pradhan Mantri Kisan Urja Suraksha evam Utthaan Mahabhiyan (PM-KUSUM)

Ministry / Department:
Ministry of New and Renewable Energy, Government of India.

Purpose:
PM-KUSUM promotes solar energy use in agriculture through solar pumps, solarisation of grid-connected pumps, and decentralised solar power plants.

Benefits:
- Subsidy support for eligible standalone solar agriculture pumps.
- Solarisation support for grid-connected agriculture pumps where implemented.
- Farmers may earn income through decentralised solar power generation under applicable components.
- Reduces diesel/electricity dependence and irrigation energy costs.

Eligibility:
- Farmers, farmer groups, cooperatives, panchayats, FPOs, and water user associations may be eligible depending on component.
- Availability depends on state implementing agency targets and guidelines.
- Land/pump/electricity connection details may be required.

Documents Commonly Required:
- Aadhaar card.
- Land documents.
- Bank passbook.
- Existing pump/electricity connection details where applicable.
- Photograph.
- Mobile number.

Where to Apply:
- State renewable energy development agency.
- DISCOM/agriculture department channels where notified.
- Official information: https://mnre.gov.in

Common Citizen Issues:
- Fake websites asking for payment.
- Subsidy application pending.
- Vendor installation delay.
- Pump capacity mismatch.
- State quota not available.

RTI Department / Office:
Public Information Officer, Ministry of New and Renewable Energy. Local records may be held by state renewable energy agency or DISCOM.

Official Source:
https://mnre.gov.in
