Scheme Name: Kisan Credit Card (KCC)

Ministry / Department:
Department of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, Government of India, implemented through banks and financial institutions.

Purpose:
Kisan Credit Card provides timely and flexible credit support to farmers for crop production and allied activities.

Benefits:
- Short-term credit for crop cultivation and working capital needs.
- Can support allied activities such as animal husbandry and fisheries where eligible.
- Interest subvention benefits may apply as per government rules and timely repayment.
- Reduces dependence on informal high-interest credit.

Eligibility:
- Farmers, tenant farmers, sharecroppers, oral lessees, and self-help/joint liability groups may be eligible as per bank norms.
- Fishers and animal husbandry farmers may be eligible under specific KCC categories.
- Land/crop/activity records and bank assessment are required.

Documents Commonly Required:
- Aadhaar card.
- Land records / tenancy proof / cultivation certificate.
- Bank passbook.
- Crop details.
- Photograph.
- Mobile number.

Where to Apply:
- Bank branch.
- PACS / cooperative bank where available.
- Agriculture department camps.
- PM-Kisan portal links and bank channels where enabled.

Common Citizen Issues:
- Loan limit not sanctioned.
- Land record mismatch.
- Interest subvention not applied.
- Renewal or enhancement delay.
- Bank asking for additional documents.

RTI Department / Office:
Public Information Officer, Department of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare. Loan-specific records are held by the concerned bank.
