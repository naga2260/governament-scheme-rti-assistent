Scheme Name: Stand-Up India

Ministry / Department:
Department of Financial Services, Ministry of Finance, Government of India.

Purpose:
Stand-Up India facilitates bank loans for SC/ST and women entrepreneurs for greenfield enterprises.

Benefits:
- Bank loan support for eligible greenfield enterprises in manufacturing, services, trading, or activities allied to agriculture.
- Focus on at least one SC/ST borrower and at least one woman borrower per bank branch.
- Helps first-time entrepreneurs access institutional credit.
- Handholding support may be available through the portal.

Eligibility:
- SC/ST and/or woman entrepreneur above 18 years.
- Greenfield project in eligible sector.
- Borrower should not be in default to any bank/financial institution.
- For non-individual enterprises, controlling stake conditions apply as per scheme rules.

Documents Commonly Required:
- Aadhaar card.
- Caste certificate for SC/ST applicant.
- Business/project report.
- Bank statements.
- Address and identity proof.
- Entity registration documents.
- Quotation/estimate for project assets.

Where to Apply:
- Bank branch.
- Stand-Up India portal: https://www.standupmitra.in
- SIDBI/NABARD handholding agencies where available.

Common Citizen Issues:
- Bank rejects project without clear reason.
- Collateral or margin money confusion.
- Project report not accepted.
- Portal application pending.
- Handholding support not received.

RTI Department / Office:
Public Information Officer, Department of Financial Services. Loan-specific records are held by the concerned bank.

Official Source:
https://www.standupmitra.in
