Scheme Name: Udyam Registration for MSMEs

Ministry / Department:
Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises, Government of India.

Purpose:
Udyam Registration provides official MSME registration for eligible micro, small, and medium enterprises.

Benefits:
- Free online MSME registration.
- Helps enterprises access MSME schemes, credit support, tenders, delayed payment protections, and other benefits where applicable.
- Permanent registration number and e-certificate.
- Linked with PAN/GST data as per rules.

Eligibility:
- Enterprise must meet MSME classification criteria based on investment and turnover.
- Proprietorship, partnership, company, LLP, SHG, cooperative, trust, or other eligible entity can register as applicable.
- Aadhaar/PAN/GST details are used as per registration rules.

Documents Commonly Required:
- Aadhaar number of proprietor/authorised signatory.
- PAN.
- GSTIN where applicable.
- Business details.
- Bank details.
- Social category and activity details.

Where to Apply:
- Official portal: https://udyamregistration.gov.in

Common Citizen Issues:
- Fake paid registration websites.
- PAN/GST validation failure.
- Wrong NIC/activity code.
- Certificate not downloaded.
- Updating enterprise details.

RTI Department / Office:
Public Information Officer, Ministry of MSME. Registration records are maintained through the Udyam portal.

Official Source:
https://udyamregistration.gov.in
