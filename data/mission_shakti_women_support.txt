Scheme Name: Mission Shakti - Women Support Services

Ministry / Department:
Ministry of Women and Child Development, Government of India.

Purpose:
Mission Shakti provides integrated support, safety, protection, rehabilitation, and empowerment services for women through components such as One Stop Centre, Women Helpline, Shakti Sadan, Sakhi Niwas, Palna, and related services.

Benefits:
- Women affected by violence can access support through One Stop Centres.
- Services may include emergency response, medical assistance, police facilitation, legal aid, counselling, and temporary shelter as applicable.
- Women Helpline 181 provides referral and support services in many states and union territories.
- Sakhi Niwas provides safe and affordable accommodation for working women where available.
- Palna and related components support childcare and women workforce participation where implemented.

Eligibility:
- Women in distress, women affected by violence, working women, and women requiring local support services may be eligible depending on the Mission Shakti component.
- Eligibility and documents vary by service, state, and local office.
- Emergency support should not be delayed only because documents are incomplete.

Documents Commonly Required:
- Aadhaar card or other identity proof where available.
- Address proof where required.
- Incident details, referral note, police record, or medical record where applicable.
- Employment or income proof for working women hostel services.
- Child details for childcare-related services.

Where to Apply:
- Women Helpline 181.
- Nearest One Stop Centre.
- District Women and Child Development Office.
- Police station, hospital, legal services authority, or protection officer referral where applicable.
- Official information: https://wcd.nic.in

Common Citizen Issues:
- Not knowing the nearest One Stop Centre.
- Helpline referral delay.
- Difficulty getting legal, medical, and police support coordinated.
- Lack of clarity on hostel or shelter availability.
- Need for RTI on action taken or service status.

RTI Department / Office:
Public Information Officer, Ministry of Women and Child Development. Local service records may be held by District Women and Child Development Office, One Stop Centre, Women Helpline unit, or State WCD Department.

Official Source:
https://wcd.nic.in
