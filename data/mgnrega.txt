Scheme Name: Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act (MGNREGA / Mahatma Gandhi NREGA)

Ministry / Department:
Ministry of Rural Development, Government of India.

Purpose:
MGNREGA enhances livelihood security of rural households by providing a legal guarantee of wage employment for adult members who volunteer to do unskilled manual work.

Benefits:
- At least 100 days of guaranteed wage employment in a financial year to every rural household whose adult members volunteer for unskilled manual work.
- Job card for registered rural households.
- Wages are paid as per notified wage rate through bank or post office account.
- Work should generally be provided within the time specified under the Act after demand for work.
- Provides transparency through muster rolls, job cards, and public reports.

Eligibility:
- Rural household.
- Adult members willing to do unskilled manual work.
- Household should register and obtain a job card through Gram Panchayat.

Documents Commonly Required:
- Application for job card.
- Aadhaar Card, if required by local implementation rules.
- Ration card or proof of residence.
- Bank or post office account details.
- Photograph of adult applicants.

Where to Apply / Check:
- Official portal: https://nrega.nic.in
- Gram Panchayat.
- Mandal / Block Development Office.
- Programme Officer under MGNREGA.

Common Citizen Issues:
- Job card not issued.
- Work demanded but not provided.
- Wage payment delayed.
- Name missing from muster roll.
- Wrong attendance or fewer workdays recorded.
- Bank account or Aadhaar seeding issue.

RTI Department / Office:
Public Information Officer, Ministry of Rural Development. Local records are usually held by Gram Panchayat, Programme Officer, Block Development Office, District Programme Coordinator, or State Rural Development Department.

Official Source:
