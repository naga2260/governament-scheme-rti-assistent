Scheme Name: Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN)

Ministry / Department:
Department of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, Government of India.

Purpose:
PM-KISAN is a Central Sector scheme that gives income support to eligible landholding farmer families. The support is meant to help farmers meet agriculture and household needs.

Benefits:
- Rs. 6,000 per year is provided to eligible farmer families.
- The amount is paid in three equal installments of Rs. 2,000.
- Payment is transferred directly to the beneficiary bank account through DBT.

Eligibility:
- Eligible beneficiary is a landholding farmer family.
- For the scheme, family means husband, wife, and minor children.
- State Governments and Union Territory administrations identify eligible farmer families as per scheme guidelines.
- Aadhaar and bank account details are important for receiving benefits.

Major Exclusions:
- Institutional landholders are not eligible.
- Farmer families with members holding constitutional posts are excluded.
- Former/present Ministers, MPs, MLAs, Mayors, and District Panchayat Chairpersons are excluded.
- Serving or retired Government employees are generally excluded, except Multi Tasking Staff, Class IV, or Group D employees.
- Pensioners receiving monthly pension of Rs. 10,000 or more are excluded, except eligible Group D type employees.
- Income tax payers and registered professionals such as doctors, engineers, lawyers, chartered accountants, and architects are excluded.

Documents Commonly Required:
- Aadhaar Card.
- Land ownership / pattadar passbook / land record.
- Bank passbook or bank account details.
- Mobile number.
- Farmer registration details, if available.

Where to Apply / Check Status:
- Official portal: https://pmkisan.gov.in
- Farmers Corner on PM-KISAN portal.
- Common Service Centre (CSC).
- Local agriculture office / revenue office as applicable in the State.

Common Citizen Issues:
- Installment not received.
- eKYC pending.
- Bank account not seeded with Aadhaar.
- Land record verification pending.
- Name mismatch between Aadhaar, bank, and land record.
- Benefit withheld due to suspected exclusion category or physical verification.

RTI Department / Office:
Public Information Officer, Department of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, Government of India. State Agriculture Department or District Agriculture Officer may hold beneficiary verification records.
