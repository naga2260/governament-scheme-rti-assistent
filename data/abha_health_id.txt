Scheme Name: Ayushman Bharat Health Account (ABHA)

Ministry / Department:
National Health Authority, Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, Government of India.

Purpose:
ABHA provides a digital health account number that helps citizens link and access health records digitally under the Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission.

Benefits:
- Unique digital health account number.
- Helps link prescriptions, lab reports, discharge summaries, and other health records with consent.
- Supports digital health services and record sharing across participating facilities.
- Free voluntary service for citizens.

Eligibility:
- Any citizen can create ABHA voluntarily.
- Aadhaar or other approved identity/mobile verification may be used.
- Use of linked health records requires consent.

Documents Commonly Required:
- Aadhaar card or other supported identity.
- Mobile number.
- Basic demographic details.

Where to Apply:
- ABHA portal: https://abha.abdm.gov.in
- ABDM Health Records app.
- Participating hospitals, health facilities, and digital health apps.

Common Citizen Issues:
- OTP not received.
- Mobile number mismatch.
- Duplicate ABHA confusion.
- Difficulty linking health records.
- Consent or privacy doubts.

RTI Department / Office:
Public Information Officer, National Health Authority. Facility-specific records are held by the concerned hospital/health provider.

Official Source:
https://abha.abdm.gov.in and https://abdm.gov.in
