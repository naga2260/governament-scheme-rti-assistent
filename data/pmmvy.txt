Scheme Name: Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana (PMMVY)

Ministry / Department:
Ministry of Women and Child Development, Government of India.

Purpose:
PMMVY provides maternity benefit support to pregnant women and lactating mothers for partial compensation of wage loss and to encourage health-seeking behaviour before and after childbirth.

Benefits:
- Cash incentive of Rs. 5,000 for the first child in two instalments.
- First instalment of Rs. 3,000 after Ante-Natal Check-up.
- Second instalment of Rs. 2,000 after childbirth and completion of 14 weeks of universal immunization.
- Under Mission Shakti, Rs. 6,000 is provided for second child if the second child is a girl, in one instalment after birth and 14 weeks immunization, subject to rules.
- Benefit is paid through DBT to bank/post office account.
- Beneficiary may also be eligible for Janani Suraksha Yojana benefits after institutional delivery as per health scheme rules.

Eligibility:
- Pregnant women and lactating mothers meeting PMMVY eligibility categories.
- Categories include SC/ST women, women with at least 40% disability, BPL ration card holders, PM-JAY beneficiaries, e-Shram card holders, PM-KISAN beneficiary women farmers, MGNREGA job card holders, women with net family income below Rs. 8 lakh per annum, pregnant/lactating AWWs/AWHs/ASHAs, and NFSA ration card holders.
- Women in regular employment with Central Government, State Government, or PSU, or receiving similar maternity benefits under other law, are not eligible.

Documents Commonly Required:
- Aadhaar Card.
- Bank or post office account details.
- Mother and Child Protection card / pregnancy registration details.
- Child birth certificate where applicable.
- Immunization record.
- Eligibility proof such as ration card, BPL card, e-Shram card, PM-JAY card, MGNREGA job card, PM-KISAN proof, caste certificate, disability certificate, or income certificate as applicable.

Where to Apply:
- Anganwadi Centre.
- Health facility / ASHA or Anganwadi Worker support.
- PMMVY portal / UMANG app where available.
- Official information: https://pmmvy.wcd.gov.in and https://www.spniwcd.wcd.gov.in/pradhan-mantri-matru-vandana-yojna

Common Citizen Issues:
- Instalment not received.
- Application pending at Anganwadi or health level.
- Bank account/Aadhaar mismatch.
- Immunization record not updated.
- Eligibility document not accepted.
- Second girl child benefit confusion.

RTI Department / Office:
Public Information Officer, Ministry of Women and Child Development. Local records may be held by District Women and Child Development Office, Child Development Project Officer, or Anganwadi project office.
