Scheme Name: AICTE Pragati Scholarship for Girls

Ministry / Department:
All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), Ministry of Education, Government of India.

Purpose:
Pragati Scholarship supports girl students pursuing technical education in AICTE-approved institutions.

Benefits:
- Scholarship assistance for eligible girl students in degree or diploma technical courses as per AICTE rules.
- Supports education expenses and encourages girls in technical education.
- Benefit is processed through the official scholarship portal and AICTE verification.

Eligibility:
- Girl student admitted to first year or lateral entry in eligible AICTE-approved degree/diploma course.
- Family income ceiling applies as per AICTE scheme rules.
- Number of girl children per family and selection conditions apply as per guidelines.

Documents Commonly Required:
- Aadhaar card.
- Bank passbook.
- Income certificate.
- Admission letter.
- Bonafide certificate.
- Previous marksheet.
- Caste/category certificate where applicable.

Where to Apply:
- National Scholarship Portal: https://scholarships.gov.in
- AICTE information: https://www.aicte-india.org
- Institution verification channel.

Common Citizen Issues:
- Institution not AICTE-approved.
- Application pending institute verification.
- Bank validation failure.
- Wrong course/year details.
- Renewal delay.

RTI Department / Office:
Public Information Officer, AICTE. Local records may be held by the institution and NSP nodal authorities.

Official Source:
https://www.aicte-india.org and https://scholarships.gov.in
