Scheme Name: Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY)

Ministry / Department:
Department of Financial Services, Ministry of Finance, Government of India.

Purpose:
PMSBY provides low-cost accident insurance cover to eligible bank or post office account holders.

Benefits:
- Accident insurance cover as per scheme rules for accidental death or disability.
- Annual renewable cover with low premium.
- Premium is auto-debited from the linked bank or post office account.
- Useful for workers, students, self-employed persons, and low-income households.

Eligibility:
- Indian citizens generally in the age group of 18 to 70 years with a bank or post office savings account.
- Consent for auto-debit is required.
- Claim depends on accident/disability documentation and policy status.

Documents Commonly Required:
- Aadhaar card.
- Bank or post office account details.
- Mobile number.
- Nominee details.
- PMSBY enrolment / consent form.
- FIR, medical records, disability certificate, or death certificate for claims as applicable.

Where to Apply:
- Bank branch.
- Post office where available.
- Bank Mitra / digital banking channels where enabled.
- Official information: https://jansuraksha.gov.in

Common Citizen Issues:
- Policy not renewed due to insufficient balance.
- Claim rejected for missing accident proof.
- Nominee details not updated.
- Delay in disability certificate.
- Confusion about coverage period.

RTI Department / Office:
Public Information Officer, Department of Financial Services. Account-specific records are held by the bank/post office and insurer.

Official Source:
https://jansuraksha.gov.in
