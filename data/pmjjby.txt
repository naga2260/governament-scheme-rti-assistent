Scheme Name: Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana (PMJJBY)

Ministry / Department:
Department of Financial Services, Ministry of Finance, Government of India.

Purpose:
PMJJBY provides low-cost life insurance cover through banks and participating insurers.

Benefits:
- Life insurance cover of Rs. 2 lakh for death due to any reason, subject to scheme rules.
- Annual renewable insurance linked to a bank or post office account.
- Low annual premium auto-debited from the subscriber account.
- Provides basic financial protection to the family of the insured person.

Eligibility:
- Indian citizens generally in the age group of 18 to 50 years with a savings bank or post office account.
- Consent for auto-debit of premium is required.
- Coverage and renewal depend on premium payment and scheme terms.

Documents Commonly Required:
- Aadhaar card.
- Bank or post office savings account details.
- Mobile number.
- Nominee details.
- PMJJBY enrolment / consent form.

Where to Apply:
- Bank branch.
- Post office where available.
- Bank Mitra / digital banking channels where enabled.
- Official information: https://jansuraksha.gov.in

Common Citizen Issues:
- Premium not auto-debited.
- Policy not renewed.
- Nominee claim delay.
- Bank account mismatch.
- Confusion between PMJJBY and PMSBY.

RTI Department / Office:
Public Information Officer, Department of Financial Services. Account-specific records are held by the bank/post office and insurer.

Official Source:
https://jansuraksha.gov.in
