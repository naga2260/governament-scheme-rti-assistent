Scheme Name: Post Matric Scholarship for Scheduled Caste Students

Ministry / Department:
Department of Social Justice and Empowerment, Government of India, implemented with states and union territories.

Purpose:
The scheme provides scholarship support to eligible Scheduled Caste students studying at post-matriculation or post-secondary levels.

Benefits:
- Financial assistance for eligible tuition fees, maintenance allowance, and other approved charges as per guidelines.
- Supports continuation of higher education for SC students.
- DBT transfer to student account where applicable.

Eligibility:
- Student must belong to Scheduled Caste category.
- Student must be studying an eligible post-matric/post-secondary course.
- Family income ceiling and course/institution rules apply.
- Student should not receive another incompatible scholarship for the same purpose.

Documents Commonly Required:
- Caste certificate.
- Income certificate.
- Aadhaar card.
- Bank passbook.
- Bonafide certificate.
- Fee receipt.
- Previous marksheet.

Where to Apply:
- National Scholarship Portal: https://scholarships.gov.in
- State scholarship portal where applicable.
- Institution scholarship office.

Common Citizen Issues:
- Application pending at institute/district/state level.
- Bank account validation failure.
- Caste or income certificate mismatch.
- Scholarship amount less than expected.
- Renewal delay.

RTI Department / Office:
Public Information Officer, Department of Social Justice and Empowerment. Local verification records may be held by institution, district welfare office, and state nodal department.

Official Source:
https://socialjustice.gov.in and https://scholarships.gov.in
