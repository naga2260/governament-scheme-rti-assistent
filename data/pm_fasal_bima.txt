Scheme Name: Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)

Ministry / Department:
Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, Government of India.

Purpose:
PMFBY provides crop insurance support to farmers against crop loss or damage due to notified natural risks and unforeseen events, helping stabilize farmer income and encourage continued farming.

Benefits:
- Insurance cover for notified food crops, oilseeds, and annual commercial / horticultural crops.
- Financial support for insured farmers suffering crop loss or damage.
- Sum insured is linked to the scale of finance per hectare and area of notified crop.
- Farmer can check premium, application status, and policy information through PMFBY portal.
- Crop loss reporting and grievance support are available through Krishi Rakshak Portal and helpline.

Eligibility:
- Farmers growing notified crops in notified areas during the notified season.
- Both loanee and non-loanee farmers may be covered as per scheme notifications.
- Crop, area, season, and insurance company depend on State notification.

Documents Commonly Required:
- Aadhaar Card.
- Bank account details.
- Land record / tenancy or crop sowing certificate as applicable.
- Crop details and area sown.
- Mobile number.
- Premium payment details for non-loanee application.

Where to Apply / Check:
- Official portal: https://pmfby.gov.in
- Farmer Corner on PMFBY portal.
- Bank branch / Common Service Centre.
- State Agriculture Department / insurance company.
- Helpline for grievances and crop loss reporting: 14447, where available.

Common Citizen Issues:
- Policy not generated.
- Premium deducted but application not visible.
- Claim not received after crop loss.
- Wrong crop or area entered.
- Crop loss intimation delayed.
- Insurance company or bank not responding.

RTI Department / Office:
Public Information Officer, Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare. For local claim data, State Agriculture Department, insurance company, bank branch, or District Agriculture Office may hold records.
