Scheme Name: eSanjeevani - National Telemedicine Service

Ministry / Department:
Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, Government of India.

Purpose:
eSanjeevani provides telemedicine consultation services so citizens can access doctors remotely.

Benefits:
- Online doctor consultation from home or through health and wellness centres.
- Reduces travel and waiting time for basic medical consultation.
- Supports e-prescription generation where available.
- Useful for follow-up and primary health advice.

Eligibility:
- Citizens can use available eSanjeevani OPD services where operational.
- Availability depends on state schedules, doctor availability, and service timings.

Documents Commonly Required:
- Mobile number.
- Basic patient details.
- Existing medical records if relevant.
- ABHA may be useful where integration is available.

Where to Apply:
- eSanjeevani portal: https://esanjeevani.mohfw.gov.in
- eSanjeevani mobile app where available.
- Health and Wellness Centre assisted teleconsultation.

Common Citizen Issues:
- Doctor not available.
- Long queue.
- OTP/login issue.
- Prescription not downloaded.
- Internet or audio/video problem.

RTI Department / Office:
Public Information Officer, Ministry of Health and Family Welfare. State telemedicine records may be held by state health department.

Official Source:
https://esanjeevani.mohfw.gov.in
