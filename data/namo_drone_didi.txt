Scheme Name: Namo Drone Didi

Ministry / Department:
Department of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, Government of India, implemented with Department of Rural Development, Department of Fertilizers, Lead Fertilizer Companies, and DAY-NRLM institutions.

Purpose:
Namo Drone Didi supports selected women Self Help Groups by enabling them to provide drone-based agricultural services such as spraying liquid fertilizers and pesticides.

Benefits:
- Selected women SHGs receive support for a drone package as per operational guidelines.
- Central assistance can cover a major portion of the drone package cost, subject to official limits.
- One selected SHG member receives drone pilot and agriculture-use training as per scheme rules.
- The SHG can earn income by renting drone services to farmers.
- The scheme promotes technology use, women entrepreneurship, and rural livelihood generation.

Eligibility:
- Women Self Help Groups under DAY-NRLM may be considered.
- Selection depends on cluster demand, local agriculture service potential, and official implementation process.
- Individual citizens usually apply through SHG and local mission channels, not as standalone applicants.

Documents Commonly Required:
- SHG registration or membership details.
- Aadhaar card of selected member.
- Bank account details of SHG/member as required.
- Mobile number.
- Training and selection records.
- Local livelihood or service plan where requested.

Where to Apply:
- Village Organisation / Cluster Level Federation.
- DAY-NRLM Block Mission Office.
- Agriculture Department or Lead Fertilizer Company facilitation channel.
- Official information: https://agricoop.gov.in

Common Citizen Issues:
- SHG not selected in the first phase.
- Confusion about whether individuals can directly apply.
- Delay in training or drone delivery.
- Questions about subsidy amount, loan share, and maintenance.
- Difficulty finding the local implementing office.

RTI Department / Office:
Public Information Officer, Department of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare. Local selection and implementation records may be held by State Rural Livelihood Mission, Agriculture Department, or Lead Fertilizer Company.
