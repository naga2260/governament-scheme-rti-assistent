Scheme Name: PM-DAKSH

Ministry / Department:
Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment, Government of India.

Purpose:
PM-DAKSH provides skill development training for eligible target groups including SC, OBC, EBC, DNT, sanitation workers, and other notified groups as per scheme rules.

Benefits:
- Free skill training through approved training institutes.
- Short-term training, upskilling/reskilling, entrepreneurship development, and long-term training components may be available.
- Stipend or wage compensation may apply for eligible trainees as per guidelines.
- Supports employability and self-employment.

Eligibility:
- Applicant should belong to eligible target group under the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment.
- Age, income, category, and course-specific criteria apply.
- Aadhaar and bank account are generally required.

Documents Commonly Required:
- Aadhaar card.
- Caste/category certificate.
- Income certificate where required.
- Bank passbook.
- Education certificate.
- Photograph and mobile number.

Where to Apply:
- PM-DAKSH portal: https://pmdaksh.dosje.gov.in
- Training centres and implementing agencies.
- District social welfare office where applicable.

Common Citizen Issues:
- Course not available locally.
- Category/income certificate mismatch.
- Stipend not received.
- Certificate delay.
- Training centre quality concerns.

RTI Department / Office:
Public Information Officer, Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment. Local records may be held by training institute and implementing agency.

Official Source:
https://pmdaksh.dosje.gov.in
