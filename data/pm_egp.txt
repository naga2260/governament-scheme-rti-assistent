Scheme Name: Prime Minister's Employment Generation Programme (PMEGP)

Ministry / Department:
Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises, implemented by Khadi and Village Industries Commission (KVIC), State KVIBs, DICs, and banks.

Purpose:
PMEGP supports setting up new micro-enterprises through credit-linked subsidy assistance.

Benefits:
- Bank loan support for eligible new micro-enterprises.
- Margin money subsidy as per category, location, and project cost rules.
- Promotes self-employment in manufacturing, service, and business activities.
- Special categories may receive higher subsidy support as per guidelines.

Eligibility:
- Individual above 18 years and eligible institutions/groups as per scheme rules.
- New project only; existing units are generally not eligible.
- Educational qualification may apply for projects above certain cost.
- Bank appraisal and project approval are required.

Documents Commonly Required:
- Aadhaar card.
- PAN where applicable.
- Project report.
- Caste/category certificate where applicable.
- Rural area certificate where applicable.
- Education certificate where required.
- Bank account details.

Where to Apply:
- PMEGP portal: https://www.kviconline.gov.in/pmegpeportal
- KVIC / KVIB / District Industries Centre.
- Bank branch after application sponsorship.

Common Citizen Issues:
- Application pending at agency/bank.
- Bank loan rejected.
- Subsidy/margin money not adjusted.
- EDP training pending.
- Project category not accepted.

RTI Department / Office:
Public Information Officer, Ministry of MSME or KVIC. Loan-specific records are held by the concerned bank.

Official Source:
https://www.kviconline.gov.in/pmegpeportal
