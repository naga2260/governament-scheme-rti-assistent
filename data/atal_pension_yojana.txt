Scheme Name: Atal Pension Yojana (APY)

Ministry / Department:
Department of Financial Services, Ministry of Finance, Government of India. Administered through Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority (PFRDA) and banks/post offices.

Purpose:
APY is a pension scheme focused on workers in the unorganised sector. It provides a guaranteed minimum monthly pension after the subscriber reaches 60 years of age.

Benefits:
- Guaranteed minimum pension options of Rs. 1,000, Rs. 2,000, Rs. 3,000, Rs. 4,000, or Rs. 5,000 per month from age 60.
- Pension amount depends on age of joining and contribution amount.
- Contributions are auto-debited from the subscriber's savings bank account.
- In case of death of subscriber, spouse may continue or receive benefits as per APY rules.

Eligibility:
- Citizen of India.
- Age between 18 and 40 years at joining.
- Must have a savings bank account.
- From 1 October 2022, a person who is or has been an income-tax payer is not eligible to join APY.

Documents Commonly Required:
- Savings bank account.
- Aadhaar Card.
- Mobile number.
- APY registration form.
- Nominee details.

Where to Apply:
- Bank branch where the savings account is held.
- Post office, where APY is offered.
- Net banking / mobile banking facility of participating banks, if available.
- Official information: https://www.jansuraksha.gov.in

Common Citizen Issues:
- Contribution not debited.
- Wrong pension slab selected.
- APY PRAN not received.
- Bank account closed or insufficient balance.
- Subscriber wants exit or change of contribution frequency.

RTI Department / Office:
Public Information Officer, Department of Financial Services or PFRDA for scheme-level information. Subscriber-level account records are usually with the bank/post office and pension system.
