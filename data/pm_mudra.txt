Scheme Name: Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana (PMMY)

Ministry / Department:
Department of Financial Services, Ministry of Finance, Government of India. Implemented through banks, MFIs, NBFCs, and other lending institutions.

Purpose:
PMMY provides loans to non-corporate, non-farm small and micro enterprises for income-generating activities in manufacturing, trading, services, and allied sectors.

Benefits:
- MUDRA loans are categorized as Shishu, Kishor, and Tarun based on loan size and business stage.
- Shishu generally covers loans up to Rs. 50,000.
- Kishor generally covers loans above Rs. 50,000 and up to Rs. 5 lakh.
- Tarun generally covers loans above Rs. 5 lakh and up to Rs. 10 lakh.
- Loans can support working capital, equipment, business expansion, and micro-enterprise needs.

Eligibility:
- Non-corporate, non-farm micro or small business activity.
- Applicant may be an individual, proprietor, partnership, small manufacturing unit, shopkeeper, vendor, service provider, repair unit, transport operator, artisan, etc., subject to lender rules.
- Applicant should satisfy the lending institution's credit assessment.

Documents Commonly Required:
- Identity proof and address proof.
- Business proof / registration, if available.
- Bank account statement.
- Quotation for machinery/equipment, where applicable.
- Caste/category certificate, if applicable.
- Photograph and application form.

Where to Apply:
- Commercial banks.
- Regional Rural Banks.
- Small Finance Banks.
- Micro Finance Institutions.
- NBFCs.
- Official information: https://www.mudra.org.in

Common Citizen Issues:
- Bank rejects loan without clear reason.
- Collateral or guarantor demanded despite micro-loan category.
- Application pending for long time.
- Sanctioned amount lower than requested.
- Confusion between subsidy scheme and loan scheme.

RTI Department / Office:
For policy information, Public Information Officer, Department of Financial Services. For a specific loan application, records are with the concerned bank or lending institution.
