Scheme Name: Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY)

Ministry / Department:
Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas, Government of India.

Purpose:
PMUY provides LPG connections to women from poor households so families can shift from traditional cooking fuels such as firewood, coal, and cow-dung cakes to cleaner LPG cooking fuel.

Benefits:
- LPG connection in the name of an eligible adult woman.
- Support for LPG connection under PMUY as per scheme norms.
- Access to LPG distributor services and refill booking.
- Safety and consumer awareness support from Oil Marketing Companies.

Eligibility:
- Applicant must be a woman who has completed 18 years of age.
- There should be no other LPG connection from any Oil Marketing Company in the same household.
- Applicant must belong to a poor household based on prescribed eligibility/deprivation declaration.

Documents Commonly Required:
- KYC application form.
- Aadhaar of applicant as proof of identity.
- Address proof if current address is different from Aadhaar.
- Ration card or State Government document certifying family composition.
- Aadhaar of adult family members listed in family document.
- Bank account details / passbook copy / cancelled cheque.
- Deprivation declaration where required.

Where to Apply:
- Official portal: https://www.pmuy.gov.in
- Nearest LPG distributor.
- Online application links of IndianOil, BharatGas, or HP Gas shown on PMUY portal.

Common Citizen Issues:
- LPG connection rejected because another household connection exists.
- Aadhaar or bank details mismatch.
- Distributor asking for missing KYC documents.
- Delay in connection release.
- Difficulty locating nearest LPG distributor.

RTI Department / Office:
Public Information Officer, Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas. For local connection status, relevant Oil Marketing Company and LPG distributor office may hold records.
