Scheme Name: National Pension Scheme for Traders and Self Employed Persons

Ministry / Department:
Ministry of Labour and Employment, Government of India.

Purpose:
This pension scheme supports small traders, shopkeepers, and self-employed persons with old-age income security.

Benefits:
- Assured pension after 60 years as per scheme rules.
- Voluntary and contributory pension model.
- Government contributes matching share to eligible subscriber contributions.
- Useful for small shopkeepers, retail traders, and self-employed workers.

Eligibility:
- Trader or self-employed person generally aged 18 to 40 years.
- Annual turnover ceiling applies as per scheme rules.
- Should have Aadhaar and bank account.
- Should not be covered under certain statutory social security schemes.

Documents Commonly Required:
- Aadhaar card.
- Bank account details.
- Mobile number.
- GSTIN or self-certification of business where required.
- Nominee details.

Where to Apply:
- Common Service Centre (CSC).
- Official portal: https://maandhan.in
- Labour department facilitation offices where available.

Common Citizen Issues:
- Wrong business category.
- Auto-debit failure.
- Confusion about contribution amount.
- Account or nominee update issues.
- Eligibility mismatch due to age or turnover.

RTI Department / Office:
Public Information Officer, Ministry of Labour and Employment. Local enrolment records may be held by CSC and labour department offices.

Official Source:
https://maandhan.in
