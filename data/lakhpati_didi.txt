Scheme Name: Lakhpati Didi Initiative

Ministry / Department:
Ministry of Rural Development, Government of India, through Deendayal Antyodaya Yojana - National Rural Livelihood Mission (DAY-NRLM).

Purpose:
The Lakhpati Didi initiative supports women Self Help Group members to build sustainable livelihoods and reach an annual household income of Rs. 1 lakh or more.

Benefits:
- Livelihood planning support for women SHG members.
- Skill building, enterprise support, and handholding through NRLM institutions.
- Credit linkage and convergence with other government schemes.
- Support for activities such as agriculture, livestock, food processing, services, and micro-enterprises.
- Encourages financial independence and stronger participation of rural women in local economies.

Eligibility:
- Woman should be a member of a Self Help Group under DAY-NRLM or a linked state rural livelihood mission.
- Household livelihood plan and income potential are considered by local mission teams.
- Specific support depends on state implementation, SHG grading, credit history, and chosen livelihood.

Documents Commonly Required:
- SHG membership proof.
- Aadhaar card.
- Bank passbook.
- Livelihood or enterprise plan.
- Income and activity details.
- Caste, income, or special category certificate where required by linked schemes.

Where to Apply:
- Self Help Group meeting.
- Village Organisation or Cluster Level Federation.
- DAY-NRLM Block Mission Management Unit.
- Official information: https://nrlm.gov.in

Common Citizen Issues:
- Not knowing whether the SHG is linked to NRLM.
- Delay in credit linkage or enterprise support.
- Confusion between target income and direct cash benefit.
- Difficulty getting livelihood plan approved.
- Lack of training access in the local cluster.

RTI Department / Office:
Public Information Officer, Ministry of Rural Development. Local records may be held by State Rural Livelihood Mission, District Mission Management Unit, or Block Mission Office.
