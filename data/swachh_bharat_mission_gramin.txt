Scheme Name: Swachh Bharat Mission - Grameen (SBM-G)

Ministry / Department:
Department of Drinking Water and Sanitation, Ministry of Jal Shakti, Government of India.

Purpose:
SBM-G promotes rural sanitation, toilet access, solid and liquid waste management, and ODF Plus villages.

Benefits:
- Support for individual household latrine construction for eligible rural households as per guidelines.
- Community sanitation and waste management activities.
- Supports ODF sustainability and village cleanliness.
- Improves health, dignity, and sanitation access.

Eligibility:
- Rural households eligible under SBM-G guidelines and local beneficiary list.
- Gram Panchayat and district verification are required.
- Support depends on state implementation and fund availability.

Documents Commonly Required:
- Aadhaar card.
- Bank passbook.
- Household/residence proof.
- Photograph of toilet construction where required.
- Gram Panchayat verification.

Where to Apply:
- Gram Panchayat.
- Block development office.
- District sanitation office.
- Official portal: https://swachhbharatmission.gov.in

Common Citizen Issues:
- Toilet incentive not received.
- Beneficiary name missing.
- Geo-tagging/photo upload issue.
- Toilet built but payment pending.
- Waste management work not completed.

RTI Department / Office:
Public Information Officer, Department of Drinking Water and Sanitation. Local records may be held by Gram Panchayat, Block Office, and District Sanitation Office.

Official Source:
https://swachhbharatmission.gov.in
