Scheme Name: Post Matric Scholarship Scheme for Minorities

Ministry / Department:
Ministry of Minority Affairs, Government of India.

Purpose:
The scheme supports eligible minority community students in post-matric education, subject to current government guidelines and availability.

Benefits:
- Scholarship support for eligible students from notified minority communities.
- Assistance may cover admission/tuition fee and maintenance allowance as per scheme rules.
- Encourages continuation of education beyond class 10.

Eligibility:
- Student should belong to a notified minority community.
- Student should be studying in an eligible course/institution.
- Family income ceiling and academic performance criteria apply as per current rules.
- Application and verification are done through the scholarship portal/process in force.

Documents Commonly Required:
- Community certificate / self declaration as required.
- Income certificate.
- Aadhaar card.
- Bank passbook.
- Bonafide certificate.
- Previous marksheet.
- Fee receipt where applicable.

Where to Apply:
- National Scholarship Portal: https://scholarships.gov.in where open.
- Institution and state/UT nodal verification channels.
- Ministry information: https://minorityaffairs.gov.in

Common Citizen Issues:
- Portal application not open.
- Application pending verification.
- Bank account validation failure.
- Income/community document mismatch.
- Scholarship not credited.

RTI Department / Office:
Public Information Officer, Ministry of Minority Affairs. Local records may be held by institution and state/UT nodal departments.

Official Source:
https://minorityaffairs.gov.in and https://scholarships.gov.in
