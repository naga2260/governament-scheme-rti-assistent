Scheme Name: PM Street Vendor's AtmaNirbhar Nidhi (PM SVANidhi)

Ministry / Department:
Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs, Government of India.

Purpose:
PM SVANidhi provides affordable working capital loans and digital transaction incentives to street vendors affected by livelihood disruption and needing business restart or expansion support.

Benefits:
- Initial working capital loan up to Rs. 10,000.
- On timely repayment, vendors can become eligible for higher subsequent loans as per scheme guidelines.
- Interest subsidy is available for eligible timely repayment.
- Cashback incentives are available for eligible digital transactions.
- Helps street vendors build credit history and formal financial access.

Eligibility:
- Street vendors engaged in vending in urban areas, subject to scheme cut-off and identification rules.
- Vendors with Certificate of Vending / Identity Card issued by Urban Local Body are covered.
- Vendors identified in survey but not issued certificate/ID may apply as per rules.
- Other categories may be covered through Letter of Recommendation process as per scheme guidelines.

Documents Commonly Required:
- Aadhaar Card.
- Mobile number linked with Aadhaar.
- Bank account details.
- Certificate of Vending / Identity Card / Letter of Recommendation, as applicable.
- Vending activity details.

Where to Apply:
- Official portal: https://pmsvanidhi.mohua.gov.in
- Urban Local Body.
- Common Service Centre.
- Lending bank / microfinance institution.

Common Citizen Issues:
- Loan application pending.
- Letter of Recommendation not issued.
- Bank rejects application.
- Interest subsidy not credited.
- Cashback not received.
- Mobile/Aadhaar authentication problem.

RTI Department / Office:
Public Information Officer, Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs. Local records may be held by Urban Local Body, lending institution, or State Urban Development Department.
