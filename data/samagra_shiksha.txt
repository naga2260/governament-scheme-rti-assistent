Scheme Name: Samagra Shiksha

Ministry / Department:
Department of School Education and Literacy, Ministry of Education, Government of India.

Purpose:
Samagra Shiksha is an integrated school education programme covering pre-school to class 12, focused on access, equity, quality, teacher support, and inclusive education.

Benefits:
- Support for school infrastructure, teacher training, learning materials, and quality improvement.
- Inclusive education support for children with special needs.
- Support for girls education, residential schools, digital initiatives, and vocational education depending on state plan.
- Strengthens government and aided school education systems.

Eligibility:
- Benefits are usually delivered through schools, state education departments, and approved annual work plans.
- Students, teachers, and schools receive support based on component-specific rules.

Documents Commonly Required:
- School enrolment records.
- Student identity details.
- Disability certificate for special needs support where applicable.
- Bank account details for DBT components where applicable.
- Caste/income certificate where required by specific component.

Where to Apply:
- Government school / headmaster.
- Mandal/block education office.
- District education office.
- State education department portal where available.

Common Citizen Issues:
- Scholarship/support not received.
- Uniforms, textbooks, transport, or special educator support delayed.
- School infrastructure work pending.
- Child with special needs not receiving aids/appliances.
- Name or bank details mismatch.

RTI Department / Office:
Public Information Officer, Ministry of Education. Local records are held by State Education Department, District Education Office, and schools.
