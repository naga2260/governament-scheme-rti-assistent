Scheme Name: Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (AB PM-JAY)

Ministry / Department:
National Health Authority, Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, Government of India.

Purpose:
AB PM-JAY is a public health assurance scheme that provides health cover for eligible poor and vulnerable families for secondary and tertiary care hospitalization.

Benefits:
- Health cover up to Rs. 5 lakh per eligible family per year.
- Covers hospitalization for listed procedures in empanelled public and private hospitals.
- Cashless and paperless treatment at empanelled hospitals.
- Family size and age restrictions are generally not applied under PM-JAY entitlement.

Eligibility:
- Eligibility is based on deprivation and occupational criteria from official beneficiary databases used by the scheme.
- Beneficiaries can check eligibility through official PM-JAY channels, Ayushman card services, Common Service Centres, or empanelled hospital help desks.

Documents Commonly Required:
- Aadhaar Card or other accepted identity document.
- Ration card / family ID / beneficiary identification document where applicable.
- Mobile number.
- Existing Ayushman card, if already generated.

Where to Apply / Check Eligibility:
- Official portal: https://pmjay.gov.in
- Mera PM-JAY services.
- Common Service Centre (CSC).
- Ayushman Mitra help desk at empanelled hospitals.
- State Health Agency / District Implementation Unit.

Common Citizen Issues:
- Name not found in beneficiary list.
- Ayushman card not generated.
- Hospital refuses cashless treatment.
- Claim or treatment package confusion.
- Aadhaar or family details mismatch.

RTI Department / Office:
Public Information Officer, National Health Authority, Ministry of Health and Family Welfare. For state-level beneficiary records, contact the State Health Agency or District PM-JAY office.
