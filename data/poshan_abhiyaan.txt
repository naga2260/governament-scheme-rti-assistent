Scheme Name: POSHAN Abhiyaan

Ministry / Department:
Ministry of Women and Child Development, Government of India.

Purpose:
POSHAN Abhiyaan aims to improve nutritional outcomes for children, adolescent girls, pregnant women, and lactating mothers through convergence, monitoring, and behaviour change.

Benefits:
- Nutrition monitoring and support through Anganwadi services.
- Focus on reducing stunting, undernutrition, anaemia, and low birth weight.
- Growth monitoring and nutrition counselling.
- Convergence with health, sanitation, drinking water, and food security services.

Eligibility:
- Children, adolescent girls, pregnant women, and lactating mothers served through Anganwadi and related services.
- Benefits depend on local Anganwadi registration and service availability.

Documents Commonly Required:
- Aadhaar card where required.
- Mother and Child Protection card.
- Pregnancy or child registration details.
- Immunization and growth monitoring records.
- Local Anganwadi records.

Where to Apply:
- Anganwadi Centre.
- ASHA / Anganwadi Worker.
- Child Development Project Officer office.
- District Women and Child Development Office.

Common Citizen Issues:
- Child growth record not updated.
- Take-home ration not received.
- Pregnant woman not registered.
- Anganwadi service disruption.
- Data mismatch in POSHAN tracker.

RTI Department / Office:
Public Information Officer, Ministry of Women and Child Development. Local records may be held by Anganwadi Centre, CDPO office, and District WCD office.
