Scheme Name: Janani Suraksha Yojana (JSY)

Ministry / Department:
Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, Government of India, under National Health Mission.

Purpose:
JSY promotes institutional delivery among pregnant women, especially poor and vulnerable women, to reduce maternal and neonatal mortality.

Benefits:
- Cash assistance for eligible pregnant women for institutional delivery as per state/category rules.
- Support through ASHA for antenatal care, institutional delivery, and postnatal care.
- Linked with public health facilities and accredited private facilities where applicable.

Eligibility:
- Pregnant women meeting JSY eligibility norms based on state category, BPL/SC/ST or other conditions as applicable.
- Delivery should take place in eligible institution or approved setting as per guidelines.
- ASHA facilitation rules vary by state.

Documents Commonly Required:
- Aadhaar card.
- Bank account details.
- Mother and Child Protection card.
- Pregnancy registration details.
- Caste/BPL/ration card where applicable.
- Delivery record / discharge summary.

Where to Apply:
- Government health facility.
- ASHA / ANM.
- Primary Health Centre / Community Health Centre.
- District health office.

Common Citizen Issues:
- JSY payment delayed.
- Bank account/Aadhaar mismatch.
- Delivery record not updated.
- ASHA incentive pending.
- Eligibility confusion by state category.

RTI Department / Office:
Public Information Officer, Ministry of Health and Family Welfare. Local records may be held by PHC/CHC, district health office, and state NHM office.
