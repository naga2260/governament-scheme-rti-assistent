Scheme Name: PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana

Ministry / Department:
Ministry of New and Renewable Energy, Government of India.

Purpose:
PM Surya Ghar supports rooftop solar installation for residential households and aims to reduce electricity bills through solar generation.

Benefits:
- Central financial assistance for eligible residential rooftop solar systems.
- Potential reduction in monthly electricity bills.
- Net metering benefits as per DISCOM/state rules.
- Online vendor and application tracking through national portal.

Eligibility:
- Residential electricity consumer with suitable rooftop and valid electricity connection.
- Applicant must follow national portal and DISCOM approval process.
- Subsidy depends on capacity and scheme rules.

Documents Commonly Required:
- Electricity bill / consumer number.
- Aadhaar card.
- Bank account details.
- Rooftop/property details.
- Mobile number.

Where to Apply:
- National portal: https://pmsuryaghar.gov.in
- Local DISCOM approval and vendor installation process.

Common Citizen Issues:
- DISCOM feasibility approval delayed.
- Vendor installation delay.
- Subsidy not credited.
- Net meter installation pending.
- Wrong bank or electricity consumer details.

RTI Department / Office:
Public Information Officer, Ministry of New and Renewable Energy. Local records may be held by DISCOM and state renewable energy agency.

Official Source:
https://pmsuryaghar.gov.in
