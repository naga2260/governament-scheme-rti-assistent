Scheme Name: National Social Assistance Programme (NSAP)

Ministry / Department:
Ministry of Rural Development, Government of India.

Purpose:
NSAP provides social assistance to poor households for old age, widowhood, disability, death of primary breadwinner, and food security support for certain uncovered senior citizens.

Main Components:
- Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme (IGNOAPS).
- Indira Gandhi National Widow Pension Scheme (IGNWPS).
- Indira Gandhi National Disability Pension Scheme (IGNDPS).
- National Family Benefit Scheme (NFBS).
- Annapurna Scheme.

Benefits:
- IGNOAPS: Central pension contribution of Rs. 200 per month for BPL persons aged 60 to 79; Rs. 500 per month for beneficiaries aged 80 and above. State Governments may add their own contribution.
- IGNWPS: Central pension contribution of Rs. 200 per month for BPL widows aged 40 to 59; States may add contribution.
- IGNDPS: Central pension contribution for BPL persons aged 18 to 59 with severe or multiple disabilities.
- NFBS: Lump sum assistance to a BPL household on death of primary breadwinner, subject to scheme rules.
- Annapurna: 10 kg food grains per month free of cost for eligible senior citizens who remain uncovered under old age pension.

Eligibility:
- Beneficiary should belong to a household below poverty line as per scheme criteria.
- Component-specific age, widowhood, disability, or family benefit conditions apply.
- State/UT implementation rules and beneficiary lists are used for selection and payment.

Documents Commonly Required:
- Aadhaar Card.
- Age proof.
- BPL/ration card or poverty status document.
- Bank or post office account details.
- Death certificate for NFBS.
- Disability certificate for disability pension.
- Widow certificate / death certificate of spouse for widow pension.

Where to Apply:
- Gram Panchayat / Ward Secretariat.
- Mandal / Block Development Office.
- Social Welfare Department / Rural Development Department, depending on State.
- Official portal: https://nsap.nic.in

Common Citizen Issues:
- Pension stopped.
- Name missing from beneficiary list.
- Payment delayed.
- Bank account inactive or Aadhaar mismatch.
- Age/category correction required.
- Death or disability certificate pending.

RTI Department / Office:
Public Information Officer, Ministry of Rural Development. State Social Welfare/Rural Development Department, District Social Welfare Office, Block Office, or Gram Panchayat may hold beneficiary records.
