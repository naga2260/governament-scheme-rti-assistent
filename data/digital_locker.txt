Scheme Name: DigiLocker

Ministry / Department:
Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology, Government of India.

Purpose:
DigiLocker provides a secure digital document wallet for citizens to access and share issued documents and certificates.

Benefits:
- Access digital driving licence, vehicle registration, education certificates, Aadhaar-linked documents, and other issued records where available.
- Digital documents from issuers are legally accepted under applicable rules.
- Reduces need to carry physical documents.
- Allows document sharing and verification.

Eligibility:
- Citizens can register using mobile number and identity verification.
- Aadhaar linking enables access to many issued documents.

Documents Commonly Required:
- Mobile number.
- Aadhaar number where linking is used.
- Basic profile details.

Where to Apply:
- DigiLocker portal/app: https://www.digilocker.gov.in

Common Citizen Issues:
- OTP not received.
- Document not found.
- Aadhaar linking issue.
- Name/date mismatch.
- Issuer not available.

RTI Department / Office:
Public Information Officer, Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology. Document-specific records are held by issuing department/authority.

Official Source:
https://www.digilocker.gov.in
