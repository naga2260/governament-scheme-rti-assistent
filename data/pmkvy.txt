Scheme Name: Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVY)

Ministry / Department:
Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship, Government of India.

Purpose:
PMKVY provides skill training and certification to youth to improve employability and livelihood opportunities.

Benefits:
- Short-term training in job-oriented skills through approved training centres.
- Recognition of Prior Learning for people with existing skills.
- Assessment and certification support.
- Placement, counselling, and livelihood support may be available through training partners.

Eligibility:
- Indian youth, school/college dropouts, unemployed persons, or workers needing skill certification may apply depending on course.
- Aadhaar, bank account, and eligibility documents may be required.
- Course availability depends on local training centre and sector skill council.

Documents Commonly Required:
- Aadhaar card.
- Bank passbook.
- Education certificate where required.
- Photograph.
- Mobile number.
- Category certificate where applicable.

Where to Apply:
- Skill India Digital portal: https://www.skillindiadigital.gov.in
- PMKVY / Skill India training centres.
- District skill development offices.

Common Citizen Issues:
- Training centre not available nearby.
- Certificate delayed.
- Placement not provided.
- Attendance or biometric issue.
- Fake training centre claims.

RTI Department / Office:
Public Information Officer, Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship. Local training records may be held by training partner and state skill mission.

Official Source:
https://www.skillindiadigital.gov.in and https://www.msde.gov.in
