Scheme Name: PM POSHAN (Mid-Day Meal Scheme)

Ministry / Department:
Department of School Education and Literacy, Ministry of Education, Government of India.

Purpose:
PM POSHAN provides hot cooked meals to eligible school children to improve nutrition, attendance, and learning outcomes.

Benefits:
- Hot cooked meals in eligible government and government-aided schools.
- Nutrition support for children in elementary classes and other covered categories as per guidelines.
- Promotes school attendance and child nutrition.
- Includes monitoring, food grain support, cooking cost, and kitchen infrastructure components.

Eligibility:
- Children enrolled in eligible schools/classes covered under PM POSHAN.
- Delivery is through school and state education department systems.

Documents Commonly Required:
- School enrolment records.
- Student attendance records.
- Aadhaar or student ID where required by state systems.
- No separate household application is usually required for daily meal access.

Where to Apply:
- School headmaster.
- Mandal/block education office.
- District education office.
- State education department grievance channel.

Common Citizen Issues:
- Meal not served regularly.
- Poor quality or insufficient quantity.
- Food grain/cooking cost delay.
- Cook-cum-helper payment delay.
- Student name missing from school records.

RTI Department / Office:
Public Information Officer, Ministry of Education. Local records are held by school, block education office, and district education office.
