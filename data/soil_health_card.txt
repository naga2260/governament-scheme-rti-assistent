Scheme Name: Soil Health Card Scheme

Ministry / Department:
Department of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, Government of India.

Purpose:
The Soil Health Card Scheme helps farmers understand soil nutrient status and receive crop-wise fertilizer recommendations.

Benefits:
- Soil testing and soil health card generation.
- Recommendations on nutrients, fertilisers, and soil amendments.
- Helps farmers reduce unnecessary fertiliser use and improve productivity.
- Promotes balanced nutrient management.

Eligibility:
- Farmers whose soil samples are collected under the scheme through state agriculture machinery.
- Availability depends on state sampling schedule and local agriculture department implementation.

Documents Commonly Required:
- Farmer identity details.
- Aadhaar card where required.
- Land details / survey number.
- Mobile number.
- Crop details if requested.

Where to Apply:
- Local agriculture officer.
- Soil testing laboratory.
- Farmer service centre / agriculture department camp.
- Official portal: https://soilhealth.dac.gov.in

Common Citizen Issues:
- Soil sample not collected.
- Soil Health Card not generated.
- Wrong land or farmer details.
- Difficulty understanding fertilizer recommendation.
- Delay in lab testing.

RTI Department / Office:
Public Information Officer, Department of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare. Local records may be held by State Agriculture Department and soil testing laboratories.

Official Source:
https://soilhealth.dac.gov.in
