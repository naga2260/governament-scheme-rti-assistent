Scheme Name: Agriculture Infrastructure Fund (AIF)

Ministry / Department:
Department of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, Government of India.

Purpose:
AIF provides medium to long-term financing support for post-harvest management infrastructure and community farming assets.

Benefits:
- Interest subvention on eligible loans as per scheme rules.
- Credit guarantee support may be available for eligible borrowers.
- Supports warehouses, cold chains, primary processing, grading, sorting, custom hiring centres, and other agriculture infrastructure.
- Useful for FPOs, PACS, entrepreneurs, startups, SHGs, and farmers.

Eligibility:
- Eligible entities include farmers, FPOs, PACS, SHGs, JLGs, agri-entrepreneurs, startups, marketing cooperatives, and other notified beneficiaries.
- Project must fit eligible infrastructure categories.
- Bank appraisal and scheme portal approval are required.

Documents Commonly Required:
- Aadhaar / identity proof.
- Entity registration documents.
- Project report.
- Land or lease documents.
- Bank account details.
- Financial statements where required.

Where to Apply:
- AIF portal: https://agriinfra.dac.gov.in
- Bank branch / lending institution.
- Agriculture department facilitation channels.

Common Citizen Issues:
- Project category not accepted.
- Bank loan not sanctioned.
- Interest subvention delay.
- Portal approval pending.
- Missing project report or land proof.

RTI Department / Office:
Public Information Officer, Department of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare. Loan records are held by the concerned lending institution.

Official Source:
https://agriinfra.dac.gov.in
