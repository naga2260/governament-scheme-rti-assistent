Scheme Name: Pradhan Mantri Shram Yogi Maandhan (PM-SYM)

Ministry / Department:
Ministry of Labour and Employment, Government of India.

Purpose:
PM-SYM is a pension scheme for eligible unorganised workers to provide old-age social security.

Benefits:
- Assured monthly pension after 60 years as per scheme rules.
- Voluntary and contributory pension scheme.
- Government contributes matching share to the subscriber contribution.
- Supports workers such as street vendors, domestic workers, agricultural workers, construction workers, and other unorganised workers.

Eligibility:
- Unorganised worker generally aged 18 to 40 years.
- Monthly income ceiling applies as per scheme rules.
- Should not be covered under certain statutory social security schemes.
- Should have Aadhaar and savings bank account / Jan Dhan account.

Documents Commonly Required:
- Aadhaar card.
- Savings bank or Jan Dhan account details.
- Mobile number.
- Self-certification of occupation and income.

Where to Apply:
- Common Service Centre (CSC).
- Official portal: https://maandhan.in
- Labour department facilitation centres where available.

Common Citizen Issues:
- Age or income mismatch.
- Auto-debit failure.
- Wrong bank details.
- Confusion between PM-SYM and Atal Pension Yojana.
- Difficulty updating nominee or bank account.

RTI Department / Office:
Public Information Officer, Ministry of Labour and Employment. Local enrolment records may be held by CSC and labour department offices.

Official Source:
https://maandhan.in
