Scheme Name: National Career Service (NCS)

Ministry / Department:
Ministry of Labour and Employment, Government of India.

Purpose:
National Career Service provides a digital platform for job seekers, employers, counsellors, and career centres.

Benefits:
- Job seeker registration and job matching.
- Career counselling and employment-related services.
- Job fairs and employer postings.
- Access to government and private job opportunities listed on the portal.

Eligibility:
- Job seekers, students, employers, placement organisations, and counsellors can register as per portal rules.
- Some services may require identity verification and profile completion.

Documents Commonly Required:
- Aadhaar or identity details where required.
- Mobile number and email.
- Education and skill details.
- Resume / employment history.

Where to Apply:
- NCS portal: https://www.ncs.gov.in
- Model Career Centres and employment exchanges where available.

Common Citizen Issues:
- Login or OTP issue.
- Fake job concerns.
- Employer not responding.
- Profile not visible.
- Job fair registration confusion.

RTI Department / Office:
Public Information Officer, Ministry of Labour and Employment. Portal-specific support is handled by NCS helpdesk.

Official Source:
https://www.ncs.gov.in
