Scheme Name: National Livestock Mission (NLM)

Ministry / Department:
Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying, Government of India.

Purpose:
National Livestock Mission supports livestock entrepreneurship, breed improvement, feed and fodder development, and rural livelihood generation.

Benefits:
- Support for entrepreneurship in poultry, sheep, goat, piggery, feed and fodder, and related activities as per current guidelines.
- Subsidy or credit-linked support may be available for eligible projects.
- Promotes employment and income generation through livestock activities.
- Helps individuals, SHGs, FPOs, cooperatives, and entrepreneurs depending on component.

Eligibility:
- Eligibility depends on selected NLM component and state implementation.
- Applicant must submit a viable project and meet bank/state requirements.
- Land, training, experience, or entity registration may be needed.

Documents Commonly Required:
- Aadhaar card.
- Bank passbook.
- Project report.
- Land or shed details.
- Entity registration documents where applicable.
- Training/experience proof where required.

Where to Apply:
- District Animal Husbandry Office.
- State animal husbandry department.
- NLM entrepreneurship portal where enabled.
- Official information: https://dahd.nic.in

Common Citizen Issues:
- Project not approved.
- Subsidy release pending.
- Bank loan delay.
- Lack of clarity on eligible activities.
- Missing land/shed documents.

RTI Department / Office:
Public Information Officer, Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying. Local records may be held by State Animal Husbandry Department.

Official Source:
https://dahd.nic.in
