Scheme Name: Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana - Urban (PMAY-U)

Ministry / Department:
Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs, Government of India.

Purpose:
PMAY-U supports the goal of Housing for All in urban areas by helping eligible urban households access affordable housing support as per mission guidelines.

Benefits:
- Assistance under eligible PMAY-U components such as beneficiary-led construction/enhancement, affordable housing, or credit-linked support as applicable.
- Female head of family is required to be owner or co-owner under mission provisions, subject to scheme rules.
- Helps eligible urban households obtain or construct a pucca house.

Eligibility:
- Applicant household should be in an urban area covered under the mission.
- Household should not own a pucca house in the name of any family member in any part of India, subject to scheme rules.
- Income category and component-specific eligibility apply.
- For a married couple, either spouse or both jointly may be eligible for one house, subject to income and ownership criteria.

Documents Commonly Required:
- Aadhaar Card of applicant and family members.
- Income certificate / self-declaration as required.
- Bank account details.
- Address proof.
- Land/property documents for beneficiary-led construction, where applicable.
- Photograph and mobile number.

Where to Apply / Check:
- Official portal: https://pmay-urban.gov.in
- Urban Local Body / Municipal Corporation / Municipality.
- State Urban Development or Housing Department.
- Lending bank or housing finance institution for credit-linked cases.

Common Citizen Issues:
- Application pending at Urban Local Body.
- Subsidy not credited.
- Beneficiary ID not found.
- Property or ownership document issue.
- Bank has not processed interest subsidy.

RTI Department / Office:
Public Information Officer, Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs. Local application records may be held by Urban Local Body, State Level Nodal Agency, or concerned bank.

Official Source:
https://pmay-urban.gov.in
