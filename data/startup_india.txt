Scheme Name: Startup India

Ministry / Department:
Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade, Ministry of Commerce and Industry, Government of India.

Purpose:
Startup India supports eligible startups through recognition, compliance benefits, funding access information, and ecosystem support.

Benefits:
- DPIIT startup recognition for eligible entities.
- Access to startup benefits, learning resources, events, and government programmes.
- Tax and intellectual property related benefits may apply if additional conditions are met.
- Supports innovation and entrepreneurship.

Eligibility:
- Entity should be a private limited company, registered partnership firm, or LLP.
- Age of entity, turnover, innovation/scalability, and original entity conditions apply as per DPIIT rules.
- Startup should not be formed by splitting or reconstructing an existing business.

Documents Commonly Required:
- Entity registration certificate.
- PAN.
- Brief write-up about innovation/business model.
- Website/pitch deck/video where available.
- Authorisation letter where applicable.

Where to Apply:
- Startup India portal: https://www.startupindia.gov.in
- DPIIT recognition application online.

Common Citizen Issues:
- Recognition rejected due to insufficient innovation details.
- Wrong entity type.
- Tax exemption confusion.
- Certificate download issue.
- Profile update problem.

RTI Department / Office:
Public Information Officer, DPIIT, Ministry of Commerce and Industry.

Official Source:
https://www.startupindia.gov.in
