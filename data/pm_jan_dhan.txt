Scheme Name: Pradhan Mantri Jan-Dhan Yojana (PMJDY)

Ministry / Department:
Department of Financial Services, Ministry of Finance, Government of India.

Purpose:
PMJDY is the National Mission for Financial Inclusion. It ensures access to basic banking, savings, remittance, credit, insurance, and pension services at affordable cost.

Benefits:
- One basic savings bank deposit account for an unbanked person.
- No minimum balance requirement in PMJDY accounts.
- Interest is earned on deposits.
- RuPay debit card is provided to account holder.
- Accident insurance cover with RuPay card, enhanced to Rs. 2 lakh for new PMJDY accounts opened after 28 August 2018.
- Overdraft facility up to Rs. 10,000 for eligible account holders.
- PMJDY accounts can receive Direct Benefit Transfer and can be linked to PMJJBY, PMSBY, APY, and MUDRA benefits subject to eligibility.

Eligibility:
- Person not having any other bank account can open a Basic Savings Bank Deposit account under PMJDY.
- Account can be opened at a bank branch or Business Correspondent / Bank Mitra outlet.

Documents Commonly Required:
- Aadhaar Card.
- If Aadhaar is not available, other officially valid documents accepted by banks.
- Photograph.
- Mobile number.

Where to Apply:
- Any bank branch.
- Business Correspondent / Bank Mitra outlet.
- Official information: https://www.pmjdy.gov.in

Common Citizen Issues:
- RuPay card not received or inactive.
- Accident insurance claim difficulty.
- DBT not received.
- Overdraft not approved.
- Account inactive due to non-use.
- Aadhaar or mobile not linked.

RTI Department / Office:
Public Information Officer, Department of Financial Services for scheme-level information. Account-specific records are held by the concerned bank branch.
