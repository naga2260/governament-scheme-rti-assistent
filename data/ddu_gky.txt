Scheme Name: Deen Dayal Upadhyaya Grameen Kaushalya Yojana (DDU-GKY)

Ministry / Department:
Ministry of Rural Development, Government of India.

Purpose:
DDU-GKY provides skill training and placement-linked support to rural poor youth.

Benefits:
- Free skill training for eligible rural youth.
- Placement-linked training through approved project implementing agencies.
- Support may include training material, assessment, certification, boarding/lodging, and post-placement support as per norms.
- Focuses on rural poor households and disadvantaged groups.

Eligibility:
- Rural youth generally from poor households and target groups as per scheme rules.
- Age criteria and category priorities apply as per guidelines.
- Candidate must meet course-specific educational and physical requirements.

Documents Commonly Required:
- Aadhaar card.
- Rural household / SECC or poverty status proof where required.
- Education certificates.
- Bank passbook.
- Caste/category certificate where applicable.
- Photograph and mobile number.

Where to Apply:
- DDU-GKY training centre.
- State Rural Livelihood Mission.
- District Rural Development Agency / Block office.
- Official information: https://ddugky.gov.in

Common Citizen Issues:
- Training batch not available.
- Placement not given after training.
- Certificate delay.
- Hostel/food quality issue.
- Candidate selection confusion.

RTI Department / Office:
Public Information Officer, Ministry of Rural Development. Local records may be held by State Rural Livelihood Mission and training partner.

Official Source:
https://ddugky.gov.in
