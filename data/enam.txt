Scheme Name: National Agriculture Market (e-NAM)

Ministry / Department:
Department of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, Government of India.

Purpose:
e-NAM is an online trading platform that links agricultural produce markets to help farmers and traders discover better prices.

Benefits:
- Online trading and price discovery for agricultural commodities in participating mandis.
- Access to wider buyer base where mandi is integrated with e-NAM.
- Quality assaying, electronic bidding, and e-payment facilities where available.
- Helps improve transparency in agricultural marketing.

Eligibility:
- Farmers, traders, commission agents, FPOs, and other market participants registered with participating e-NAM mandis.
- Availability depends on commodity, mandi integration, and state APMC rules.

Documents Commonly Required:
- Aadhaar card or identity proof.
- Bank account details.
- Mobile number.
- Mandi registration details.
- Farmer/FPO/trader registration documents where applicable.

Where to Apply:
- Participating APMC mandi.
- e-NAM portal: https://enam.gov.in
- Mandi helpdesk or agriculture marketing office.

Common Citizen Issues:
- Mandi not integrated with e-NAM.
- Commodity not available for online trade.
- Payment delay.
- Quality assaying dispute.
- Login or registration problem.

RTI Department / Office:
Public Information Officer, Department of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare. Local mandi records are held by APMC/market committee.

Official Source:
https://enam.gov.in
