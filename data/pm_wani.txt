Scheme Name: PM-WANI

Ministry / Department:
Department of Telecommunications, Ministry of Communications, Government of India.

Purpose:
PM-WANI promotes public Wi-Fi networks through Public Data Offices to improve broadband access.

Benefits:
- Enables public Wi-Fi access through PDOs.
- Creates local entrepreneurship opportunities for small shops and service providers.
- Helps citizens access affordable internet in public locations.
- No telecom licence fee for PDOs as per framework.

Eligibility:
- Citizens can use available PM-WANI Wi-Fi services through participating apps/providers.
- Shops and entrepreneurs can become Public Data Offices through PDO aggregators as per framework.

Documents Commonly Required:
- For users: mobile number/app login as required.
- For PDOs: identity/business details as requested by PDO aggregator.
- Bank or payment details where applicable.

Where to Apply:
- Through participating PM-WANI apps and PDO aggregators.
- Official information: https://dot.gov.in

Common Citizen Issues:
- Wi-Fi hotspot not available.
- App login/payment issue.
- Slow or unstable connectivity.
- PDO onboarding confusion.
- Local support not available.

RTI Department / Office:
Public Information Officer, Department of Telecommunications. Provider-specific records are held by concerned PDOA/app provider.

Official Source:
https://dot.gov.in
