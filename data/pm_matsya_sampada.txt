Scheme Name: Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana (PMMSY)

Ministry / Department:
Department of Fisheries, Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry and Dairying, Government of India.

Purpose:
PMMSY supports fisheries and aquaculture development, fish production, post-harvest infrastructure, and fisher welfare.

Benefits:
- Financial assistance for eligible fisheries and aquaculture activities.
- Support for ponds, hatcheries, cages, cold chain, fish transport, processing, and marketing infrastructure.
- Insurance and welfare components may be available as per guidelines.
- Helps fishers, fish farmers, entrepreneurs, SHGs, FPOs, cooperatives, and fisheries groups.

Eligibility:
- Fishers, fish farmers, fisheries cooperatives, SHGs, entrepreneurs, FPOs, and other eligible entities.
- Activity and subsidy pattern depend on central/state guidelines.
- State fisheries department approval is usually required.

Documents Commonly Required:
- Aadhaar card.
- Bank passbook.
- Project proposal.
- Land/water body lease or ownership proof where required.
- Caste/category certificate where applicable.
- Fisheries registration/license where required.

Where to Apply:
- District Fisheries Office.
- State fisheries department portal.
- PMMSY portal/information: https://pmmsy.dof.gov.in

Common Citizen Issues:
- Subsidy release delay.
- Project proposal not approved.
- Lease or water body document mismatch.
- Bank loan not sanctioned.
- Beneficiary list not published clearly.

RTI Department / Office:
Public Information Officer, Department of Fisheries. Local records may be held by State Fisheries Department and District Fisheries Office.

Official Source:
https://pmmsy.dof.gov.in
